Getting started

ProSafe is your personal, private file vault. Everything you add — photos, videos, audio, documents — lives inside this app on this device, behind your lock screen.

Once you're unlocked, you land on the main grid. From there:

  • Tap the lime + button (top right) to add files
  • Tap the menu icon (top left) to open settings — theme, and resetting your access code
  • Tap the red lock icon any time to lock the vault immediately

Your vault auto-locks after a few minutes of inactivity, so you don't need to remember to lock it every time you put your phone down.

Uploading & organizing files

Tap the + button and choose any files from your device — you can select several at once. They'll appear in the grid immediately, grouped by the day they were added.

Finding things again

  • Use the search bar to filter by filename as you type
  • Tap a category pill (Images, Videos, Audio, Documents, etc.) to narrow the grid to just that type
  • The storage bar under the search box shows how much space your vault is using

Browsing & previewing

Tap any photo, video, or audio file to open the full-screen viewer. From there you can:

  • Swipe left/right, or use ← → on a keyboard, to move between files
  • Double-tap a photo to zoom in and out
  • Tap the ⓘ icon to see file details — type, size, date added
  • Use download, rename, or delete directly from the top bar

Tapping a document, archive, or other non-visual file downloads it straight away instead of opening a preview.

Selecting, saving & deleting

Long-press any file, or tap the grid icon in the top bar, to enter selection mode. From there:

  • Tap files to add or remove them from your selection
  • Use All to select everything currently visible
  • Save downloads every selected file to your device
  • Delete removes them permanently from the vault — this cannot be undone, so double-check your selection first

Customizing your theme

Open the menu → Theme & Background to choose how the vault looks:

  • Dark — the plain, clean default
  • Image Fade — a rotating set of background photos
  • Video — a looping animated background
  • Gradient — a slow, animated color flow

Your choice is remembered and applied everywhere in the app, including this page.

Image and Video themes need an internet connection the first time each background loads. After that, it's saved on your device and keeps working even offline.

Locking & resetting access

Tap the red lock icon at any time to lock the vault immediately — useful right before handing your phone to someone else.

Resetting your code

If you ever need to change your access code, go to menu → Reset Combinations. This is protected by your fingerprint or face — you'll be asked to verify before you're allowed to set a new one, so someone else picking up your unlocked phone can't quietly change your code and lock you out.

Refreshing & updates

The grid, storage bar, and category counts update instantly whenever you add, rename, or delete a file — there's nothing to manually refresh for everyday use.

If something ever looks out of sync, or you want to make sure you're on the latest version of the app itself, simply close the app fully and reopen it (or pull down to reload, if your browser supports it). Because ProSafe works offline, it may take a moment after reconnecting to fetch a newer version if one is available.

Installing as an app

When the "Install ProSafe" banner appears at the bottom of the screen, tap Install to add it to your home screen. It'll then open full-screen, like any other app — no browser address bar, and a proper app icon.

You can dismiss the banner with the × if you'd rather keep using it in the browser; you won't lose any data either way, since everything is stored the same regardless of how you open it.

Data & safety — please read

How storage actually works

ProSafe doesn't use a cloud account. Every file you add, your access code, your fingerprint registration, and your background themes are all stored locally, inside this browser, on this device — not on a remote server. That's what makes it private: nothing you add ever leaves your phone.

The flip side is that this device and this browser are the only copy. There's no automatic backup anywhere else.

What wipes your vault

Because everything lives in browser storage, certain everyday "cleanup" actions will erase it completely — your files, your access code, and your biometric setup all at once, with no way to recover them.

Avoid these
  • Your browser's "Clear browsing data" with Cookies and other site data ticked — for this site, or "All time"
  • Android Settings → Apps → (your browser) → Storage → Clear storage — this wipes stored data for every site in that browser, not just this one
  • Uninstalling ProSafe if you've installed it to your home screen, without checking first — some browsers clear an installed app's storage on removal
  • Opening the vault in an Incognito / Private window — anything added there disappears the moment that window closes
Safe to do
  • Clearing only "Cached images and files" in your browser, with Cookies and other site data left unticked — this frees up space without touching your vault
  • Using "Clear cache" (not "Clear storage") in Android's app settings
  • Restarting your phone, closing the app, or losing internet access — none of these affect what's already stored

Since there's no backup built in

If you ever need to do something riskier to your browser — a factory reset, switching phones, or a browser reinstall — use Save/Download on anything important first to copy it out to your device's normal storage or another app. Once vault storage is cleared, it's gone for good.

Limitations to know

  • No cross-device sync. Files added on one device or browser don't appear on another — each install is entirely separate.
  • No cloud backup. As covered above, everything is local. That's the tradeoff for full privacy.
  • Storage is shown against a 2GB reference point in the usage bar — your device's actual ceiling depends on available storage and your browser's own limits, and may be higher or lower.
  • Fingerprint/face protection confirms a match to any biometric enrolled on this device — not specifically to you if someone else's fingerprint is also enrolled on the same phone.
  • Requires a reasonably modern browser. The fingerprint/face reset protection specifically needs a device and browser that support platform biometrics; without that, resetting is disabled entirely rather than left unprotected.
  • This is a personal, single-user tool — it isn't built for shared accounts or multiple people accessing the same vault differently.
